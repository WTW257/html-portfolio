Tim's list of movie rankings

My Top 3 movies

Tron (1982)

A ground breaking film reknown for both its unique artistic direction and use of new techniques for VFX and Computer Animation before many other films would follow suite. Its story of traversing a unique intepretation of the inner workings of a computer system at a time before computers reached widespread mainstream adoption with its vibrant visuals make for an unforgetable movie and backed by a wonderful and distinctive musical score.

Matrix (1999)

A wonderfully crafted film that delivers on the themes of enlightenment and freedom of choice through a post-apocalyptic story that manages to shift between two different worlds perfectly. It outlines the characters motivations clearly with arcs that are meaningful and impactful which also link to the themes presented. This is all supported with a cool soundtrack and brings some of the best action sequences that many films would try to emulate years later.

The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (2001)

Brings the detailed works of Tolkien's Middle Earth to the big screen with a well directed film that captures the essence of the original story. This films' cast was one-of-a-kind with each actor embodying their respective characters masterfully, preserving the original intent of the story while also bringing their own flair to make a unique portrayal that is memorable. All of of this is in addition to the beautiful scenery utilising both CGI and real landscapes with a masterpiece of a soundtrack to bring the book to life.
